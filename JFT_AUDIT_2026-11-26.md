# JFT-Basic course audit — 26 November 2026

## Scope and checks

Audited the local JFT course and opened the deployed site in a fresh browser session. Tested the first chapter, chapters 3, 5, 15, 16, 27 and 36, and the Next chapter link. All opened. The local route test rendered every one of the 36 chapters and 7 stage reviews. The question audit checked answer indices, choice duplication, prompts and explanations in all 540 chapter questions and both 48-question mocks. The audio audit found all 938 listed recordings. These are structural checks; they do not establish that every Japanese sentence or answer is pedagogically correct.

## Lesson opening

The reported failure did not reproduce in the fresh browser session. A stale mix of course assets is a plausible cause because the page previously loaded unversioned scripts; it is not a confirmed diagnosis of the user's browser. The update gives all JFT scripts and styles one versioned URL and shows a reload action if the course fails to initialize. The local test now covers both the failed-load message and a successful load.

## What is present

- 36 chapters in 7 stages, including both kana charts (46 basic symbols each).
- 390 worked examples, about 230 vocabulary entries, and 15 checkpoint questions per chapter (540 total).
- 7 stage reviews. Each has four new source-based questions plus reused chapter questions, for 10–12 questions per review.
- Two timed 48-question mocks, each with four 12-question sections, plus 938 listed audio files.
- A recording and playback exercise in chapters after the kana introduction.

## Priority findings

1. **Reading depth is the biggest gap.** The non-kana lesson passages are short: about 32–120 Japanese characters after removing reading markup, with a median near 64. Add longer notices, messages, schedules and information-search tasks, with questions requiring learners to combine details across sentences.
2. **Stage reviews have limited fresh transfer practice.** Four questions are new in each review; the rest are drawn from chapter checks. Add more unseen sources so a high review score demonstrates comprehension rather than memory of prior options.
3. **Mock behavior is only an approximation.** The two mocks match the official exam's four sections and 60-minute scale well. The actual JFT-Basic listening section does not allow navigating back or forward to review, whereas this course presents listening questions together and does not enforce that rule. Practice scores should remain labeled as practice scores.
4. **Audio and speaking have limits.** The recorded library is present, but it uses a synthetic voice. Recording and playback help self-practice, but the course does not assess pronunciation or save recordings.
5. **Progress is local to one browser.** A learner can lose it when browser data is cleared or when switching devices. Export/import would be a useful next addition.

## Exam alignment

The Japan Foundation describes approximately 50 questions in 60 minutes across Script and Vocabulary, Conversation and Expression, Listening Comprehension, and Reading Comprehension. The course's 48-question, four-section mocks are a reasonable practice approximation, not an official simulation or score predictor. Official specification: https://www.jpf.go.jp/jft-basic/e/about/index.html

## Limits

The local checks validate rendering, question shape, available audio files and route navigation. They do not replace a complete Japanese-language editorial review, a beginner usability test on the user's phone, or a check of the user's affected browser and saved state. If lessons still fail to open after the update, capture the exact chapter URL and browser console error to identify the remaining cause.
