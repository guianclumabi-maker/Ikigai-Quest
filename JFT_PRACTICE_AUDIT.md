# JFT-Basic practice and media audit

Checked 2026-11-27. This audit separates official examples from independent practice. It does not certify the Japanese in every Ikigai Quest lesson or every question on an external site.

## Official baseline

- The [Japan Foundation test overview](https://www.jpf.go.jp/jft-basic/e/about/) describes four sections, approximately 50 questions, and 60 minutes.
- The [official FAQ](https://www.jpf.go.jp/jft-basic/e/faq/) says past test questions are not published. The public [sample](https://www.jpf.go.jp/jft-basic/sample/q01.html) runs through 11 screens, including three listening and two reading screens.
- Ikigai Quest's 48-question mocks approximate this format. A raw practice score is not the Foundation's scaled score.

## Free external practice

| Source | Listening | Reading | Audit note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [FreeTag](https://freetag.jp/en/jft-basic/index.html) | Publisher lists 200 questions and MP3 files; audio is synthetic | Publisher lists 200 questions | Good volume for unfamiliar practice. Independent material; answer-key quality has not been fully checked. |
| [Zutto Nihon](https://zuttonihon.jp/app/jft-basic/en/) | Publisher lists 100 questions; playback uses device read-aloud | Publisher lists 90 questions | Free 48-question mock. Device speech is a weak substitute for varied human voices. |
| [JFT Prep](https://jftprep.com/jft-basic-mock-test) | One free 50-question mock with two-play audio | Reading in the same mock | Additional full mocks are paid. Independent questions and score estimates. |
| [IRODORI Japanese Online Course](https://www.jpf.go.jp/jft-basic/e/support/index.html) | Official learning course with listening practice | Official learning course with reading practice | Free reference for skill practice; not a released past-paper bank. |

The providers' question counts are their own published claims. Their sites are useful for exposure to new situations, but none of their scores predicts an official result.

## Ikigai Quest checks

- Automated checks render all 36 chapters, seven stage reviews, and both 48-question mocks. Every chapter and stage review has a listening question and a rendered play button.
- Both mocks render all eight listening groups with a play button. Playback advances one group at a time and caps at two plays. Reading groups have visible source texts.
- All 938 listed JFT clips exist locally, exceed 500 bytes, and have an MP3 header. The audit also checks the 210 distinct clips referenced by chapter dialogue, question audio, stage reviews, and mock listening.
- The 24 mock listening and 24 mock reading answer keys were checked against their scripts and texts; no source-answer mismatch was found in those 48 items.

## Remaining limits

- MP3 existence and header checks do not prove pronunciation, natural intonation, or actual playback on every browser and hosting deployment.
- Mock dialogues use a synthetic voice, so they do not yet test understanding across different human speakers and accents.
- The Japanese and answer keys across all 540 chapter questions and the external banks have not had a complete human review.
