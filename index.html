The story behind the quest

Built for the moment you decide to begin.

Ikigai Quest started in the middle of a very human question: “What am I doing next?” During a midlife reset, perhaps a quarter-life crisis, I started dreaming of going to Japan and working there. At the same time, I realized I didn’t know enough Japanese to make that dream feel possible.

Learning a language takes time, steady practice, and often a lot of money. A dedicated language school can be a wonderful path, but it isn’t available to everyone. I wanted a gentler place to start: small lessons, useful examples, and room to practice without a paywall.

With AI as a helping tool, this project turns that idea into original explanations, examples, and practice activities. The goal is to use the technology thoughtfully to make the first steps easier to reach — and to keep learning itself open to everyone.

The current course begins with the foundations and reaches through N4-level material. It’s a growing learning companion, not a replacement for conversation, teachers, or the experience of living the language.

A few quick answers

Made for a calm start

Do I need to know Japanese already?

No. The course starts with script introductions and kana flashcards, then introduces everyday kanji and beginner readings.

What level does the course cover?

The path starts with kana and N5 foundations, then includes selected N4 grammar and reading material. It does not cover N3–N1 and is not a complete JLPT syllabus.

Will my progress follow me to another device?

Progress is saved in this browser on this device. To move it, open Stats in the lesson app and copy a backup code, then paste it on the other device.

How do I begin?

Open a chapter, choose a lesson, and study at your own pace. Chapters can be collapsed, and completed lessons stay available for review.

Ready to start?

Start with the sounds. Keep going at your own pace.
