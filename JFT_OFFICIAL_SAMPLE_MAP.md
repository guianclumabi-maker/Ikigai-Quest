# JFT-Basic public sample map

The Japan Foundation publishes **sample question screens**, not a bank of past test questions. The public sample runs through 11 screens. Some screens contain more than one subquestion. This map describes their tested skill and links to the original screen; it does not reproduce the official questions, artwork, readings, or audio.

| Screen | Official category | What to practise |
| --- | --- | --- |

| [02](https://www.jpf.go.jp/jft-basic/sample/q02.html) | Word usage | Select a word that fits a sentence. |
| [03](https://www.jpf.go.jp/jft-basic/sample/q03.html) | Kanji reading | Choose the hiragana reading of a kanji word. |

Official test overview: <https://www.jpf.go.jp/jft-basic/e/about/>. The test has four sections, around 50 questions and 60 minutes. The [official FAQ](https://www.jpf.go.jp/jft-basic/e/faq/) says past test questions are not published. Its listening sample uses audio and its reading sample includes graphics; use the linked original pages to experience those elements.

## How Ikigai Quest applies this map

- The 36 chapter checkpoints cover vocabulary, kanji, grammar, expressions, reading and listening.
- Seven stage reviews now include extra original everyday readings and comprehension questions.
- Mock A and B each have 12 questions in each of the four sections. Their reading sections include notices, schedules, messages, a personal account, and an original fictional information sheet.
- Mock listening now presents one source group at a time with two plays per recording and no return to earlier groups during the attempt.

The Ikigai Quest material is independently written practice. It does not use official JFT-Basic questions, readings, images, or audio. Mock results are raw practice scores, not the official scaled score.
